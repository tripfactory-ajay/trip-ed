Bali, Indonesia

India's #1 short-haul outbound destination — temples, rice terraces, luxury villas, and world-class beaches.

⏱ 45 minutes 📍 Destination Module 🏆 20 pts on completion ✅ Counts as 1 annual module
🌍 Destination at a Glance
Flight from India
~5.5–7 hrs (Delhi/Mumbai direct)
Visa for Indians
Visa on Arrival — USD 35, 30 days
Currency
IDR — approx ₹1 = IDR 170
Best Season
Apr–Oct (dry). Peak: July–Aug
Avoid
Nov–Feb (wet season, afternoon rain)
Avg Package Length
5N/6D · 7N for families
📍 Key Areas
  • Seminyak & Legian — Beach, rooftop bars, sunset clubs, boutique hotels. Best for couples and young travellers.
  • Nusa Dua — Luxury resort strip. Five-star hotels, calm beach. Ideal for families and luxury seekers.
  • Ubud — Cultural heart. Temples, rice terraces, yoga retreats, monkey forest. Loved by honeymooners.
  • Kuta — Budget-friendly, surf lessons, beach parties. First-time travellers and young groups.
  • Canggu — Hip digital nomad zone. Coffee shops, surf, Instagram-friendly. Young couples.
  • Uluwatu — Clifftop temples, world-class surf, dramatic views. Adventurous couples.
  • Lembongan Island — Day trip or 1–2N extension. Snorkelling, manta rays, no traffic.
💡 Top Tip for Indian Customers

Always reassure early: "Is there a language barrier?" — No. Bali is extremely tourist-friendly, staff speak basic English/Hindi, signage is clear. This one reassurance removes the most common hesitation.

⚠️ Nyepi — Day of Silence (March)

Entire island shuts down — no flights, no road movement for 24 hours. Always check the exact Nyepi date for March bookings and advise customers accordingly.

💬 Objection Handling
Objection: "Bali is just for honeymooners"

"Actually Bali is one of the most versatile destinations in the world. We've done family groups, office trips, anniversary celebrations, and pilgrimages to Hindu temples. Let me show you what a family itinerary looks like — Ubud cultural day, Nusa Dua beach resort, Lembongan island — it's completely different from a honeymoon package."

Objection: "We were thinking Maldives instead"

"Maldives is beautiful but you're paying a premium for beach and water villa. For the same budget in Bali you get beach, culture, temples, wellness, waterfalls, and incredible food. Many customers do Maldives for their 10th anniversary and Bali for the honeymoon — shall I compare both side by side?"

Objection: "Is vegetarian food available?"

"Absolutely. Bali has dedicated Indian restaurants throughout Seminyak and Nusa Dua. Most major hotels also offer Indian veg menus on request — I flag this at the time of booking, so you don't need to worry at all."

Thailand

Land of Smiles — visa-free for Indians. Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai and stunning islands for every budget and traveller type.

📍 Key Destinations
  • Bangkok — Grand Palace, Chatuchak market, Chinatown, rooftop bars. 2–3 nights as base.
  • Phuket — Beach destination. Patong for nightlife, Kata/Karon for families, Kamala for luxury. Nov–April only.
  • Chiang Mai — Temples, elephant sanctuaries, night bazaars. Cultural alternative for repeat visitors.
  • Krabi / Ao Nang — Limestone cliffs, quiet beaches, rock climbing, island-hopping. Add-on to Phuket.
  • Koh Samui — Upmarket island. Good for luxury couples and families seeking quieter beach.
  • Koh Phi Phi — Stunning but smaller. Day trip or 1–2N. Popular with young travellers.
💡 Lead With Visa

Thailand is visa-free for Indians — no cost, no appointment. This is often the deciding factor vs Bali (VoA USD 35) or Sri Lanka. Always lead with this point in your pitch.

📍 Key Destinations

Sales & WhatsApp Selling

The most important skill for a TripFactory PSA. Conversation frameworks, follow-up sequences, objection handling and closing techniques.

⏱ 60 minutes 💼 Skills Module 🏆 25 pts
🎯 The TripFactory Sales Conversation Framework
1
Qualify First — Don't Quote First
Before saying anything about price, ask: Who is travelling? When? What budget? What's the occasion? Understanding takes 5 minutes. Quoting without understanding takes 20 minutes and usually leads to no sale.
2
The 3-Question Opener
"Who are you travelling with? What kind of experience — beach, culture, adventure, or a mix? Is there a special occasion?" These three questions tell you everything to pitch the right destination and hotel.
3
Present One Option, Not Five
A customer who receives 5 packages gets confused and does nothing. Present one strong, well-matched recommendation. If they push back, offer an alternative. Start with what you believe is right for them.
4
Always End With a Clear Next Step
"I'll send the quote by 5pm." "Can you confirm the rooms?" "Shall I hold these dates 48 hours?" Never leave a conversation open-ended. Dead conversations don't convert.
5
The 72-Hour Window
Most bookings happen within 72 hours of the first serious enquiry. Use your WhatsApp sequence to maintain momentum without pressure after this window closes.
💬 Sample Conversation — Bali Honeymoon
Sample Sales ConversationEnquiry → Quote
👤Customer"Hi, we're thinking of going to Bali for our honeymoon in September. What does it cost?"
🧑‍💼Agent"Congratulations! Bali is a beautiful choice. Before I quote, may I ask — how many nights, and would you prefer a beach resort, jungle villa in Ubud, or a mix of both?"
👤Customer"Maybe 6 nights. Mostly beach but we'd like to see some temples too."
🧑‍💼Agent"Perfect. I'd suggest 4 nights Nusa Dua 5-star beachfront + 2 nights Ubud — you get the rice terraces, Tirta Gangga temple, and a spa day. That's what most honeymooners choose. Are you vegetarian, and roughly what budget per person?"
👤Customer"We eat everything. Budget maybe ₹80,000 per person including flights."
🧑‍💼Agent"Very workable. I can put together 6 nights — Nusa Dua 5-star + Ubud boutique villa, flights, transfers, daily breakfast, and a couple's spa session — comfortably within that. I'll send a full quote by 5pm. What's the best number to WhatsApp you?"

🛡️ The 5 Most Common Objections
Objection 1 — "It's too expensive"

"Can you tell me what budget would feel comfortable? I can usually get very close by adjusting hotel category or shifting dates. Travelling midweek or shoulder season can save ₹15,000–20,000 on the same itinerary."

Objection 2 — "Let me check with my family first"

"Of course — absolutely the right thing to do. Is there anything specific they'll want to know that I can help you answer? I can prepare a one-page summary to show them. Shall I hold dates for 48 hours while you check?"

Objection 3 — "I found it cheaper online"

"Could you share what you found? Online prices often don't include airport transfers, breakfast, or flexibility to amend. Our packages include private transfers, daily breakfast, and full human support if anything changes on the trip — that's worth something."

Objection 4 — "Not sure about dates yet"

"That's fine — I'll send the quote anyway. Most packages allow date changes up to 30 days before departure for a small admin fee. Let's agree the itinerary now and sort dates once you're clearer."

Objection 5 — "We've never travelled abroad — is it safe?"

"First-time travellers are our speciality. All packages include private airport pickup — you're met the moment you land. Hotels are all verified with English-speaking staff. You'll have my WhatsApp the entire trip — any issue, message me directly."

🔐 DPDPA 2023 — Your 5 Core Obligations
1
Collect Only What You Need
Only ask customers for information genuinely required for the booking. Every piece of extra data you hold is a liability under DPDPA.
2
Use Data Only for the Booking
Customer data collected for a TripFactory booking must be used only for that booking. You cannot use it to market competing products or share with other companies.
3
Never Share Customer Data
Do not share passport details, payment info, or personal data with any third party without explicit customer consent and TripFactory's written approval.
4
Report a Breach Within 72 Hours
If customer data is lost, stolen, or accessed without authorisation — notify TripFactory within 72 hours. Failure to report is a serious breach of your PSA Agreement and of DPDPA law.
5
Delete Data on Exit
When your PSA Agreement ends, delete all TripFactory customer data and confirm deletion in writing. On customer request, delete their data promptly.
⚠️ Real Examples of Common Violations

❌ Sending a passport copy via unencrypted WhatsApp to a hotel  ·  ❌ Keeping a customer passport spreadsheet on your personal laptop  ·  ❌ Using a customer's email to market your other business  ·  ❌ Photographing a passport and keeping it in your phone gallery

⚖️ Consumer Protection Act 2019
1
No Misleading Representations
Do not misrepresent any product, hotel, itinerary, or inclusion. If you promise "ocean view" and the customer arrives to find garden view, you are personally liable. Confirm all inclusions in writing before collecting payment.
2
Written Confirmation Always
Every booking must have a written TripFactory confirmation showing inclusions, dates, prices, and cancellation terms. A verbal agreement is not sufficient.
3
Tell Customers Cancellation Terms Before They Pay
Customers have the right to know the cancellation and refund policy before paying. Always share TripFactory's policy and get confirmation they have read it.
4
Travel Insurance — Mandatory Advice
You must advise every customer to take travel insurance. This is a contractual obligation. If a customer declines, note it in writing. A customer without insurance who has a medical emergency may attempt to claim against you.
📊 GST Basics for PSA Partners
1
Your Commission is Subject to GST
TripFactory pays you Commission as a service fee. If GST-registered, raise a GST invoice at 18%. TripFactory will deduct TDS under the Income Tax Act 1961 before paying your Commission.
2
Register for GST if Turnover Exceeds Threshold
If annual Commission income exceeds ₹20 lakhs (₹10 lakhs in some states), you must register for GST. Confirm with a CA for your specific situation.
3
Never Collect Payments Outside TripFactory's System
All customer payments must flow through TripFactory's approved channels. Accepting cash or bank transfers into your personal account for TripFactory bookings creates both a tax liability and a contract breach — this has led to PSA terminations.
💡 Practical Tip

Keep a simple monthly spreadsheet of all Commission payments received with TripFactory reference numbers. Makes GST filing straightforward and lets you reconcile your TDS certificate at year-end.
